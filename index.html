WHERE SHORTLISTED IDEAS BECOME REAL PRODUCTS.

During Month 2 of She Builds and across flagship Grevix programs, shortlisted creators unlock full access to the Grevix Innovation Lab — bridging college innovation with seasoned industry engineering.

SHARED OWNERSHIP CHARTER VERIFIED TERMS

Project ownership is shared transparently between the creator and Grevix under clearly defined terms, with permanent credit for every team member who designs, codes, tests, and ships the project.
